Tables

Data Table

Tables display detailed information in rows and columns. They allow users to compare exact values across multiple data records.

Sample Video Performance
Video Views Watch Time
Video A 12,500 320 hours
Video B 9,800 245 hours
Video C 7,200 180 hours
Video D 5,600 140 hours

Table Styling

  • Table Header: Pastel purple (#E1CBF6).
  • Data Rows: Alternating light backgrounds.
  • Hover State: Pastel blue (#CBE1F6).
  • Text: Dark blue (#11268D).
  • Responsive: Horizontal scrolling on smaller screens.
HTML Pattern
<div class="ctv-table-wrapper">
  <table class="ctv-data-table">
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th scope="col">Video</th>
        <th scope="col">Views</th>
        <th scope="col">Watch Time</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr>
        <td>Video A</td>
        <td>12,500</td>
        <td>320 hours</td>
      </tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</div>
